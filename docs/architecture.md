# How v2's code fits together

## State and derived data
- `world.state` is the whole save: clock, money, rating, ticket, the grid, `exhibits`, `openPens`, `buildings`, `animals`, `staff`, `guests`, `jobs`, `testing` (fence tile -> hours tested), `power`, `bell`, `books` (today and last day).
- `world.derived` is rebuilt from the state and never saved: `exOf` (tile -> exhibit id, 0 outside), `regions`, and `ex` (exhibit id -> its tally).
- `world.changed()` rebuilds derived data after any grid change; `hourly()` rebuilds it too, because fences wear and get tested.

## The grid (grid.js)
Typed-array layers, one entry a tile: `water` (none, shallow, deep), `struct` (none, path, fence, building), `fence` (type index + 1), `cond` (0 to 100), `flags` (gate, chute), `bld` (building id), `ex` (the exhibit the tile last belonged to). `walkable(g, i, who)` says who may stand where (paths.js routes guests and staff on the four-way grid; animals also step diagonally, never between two blocked tiles): guests on paths only; staff anywhere but buildings, deep water and fences (gates excepted); `land`, `swim` and `both` animals by water.

## Bulldozing (world.js)
One tool takes everything away and gives back `REFUND` (25%) of what it cost (`PRICE` in config.js, fence and building prices in data.js), booked as `books.sold`. `bulldozePlan(tiles)` is read only and says what would go: fence and path tiles, water, and any building with a tile in the box (whole); a single tile on a gate or chute takes just that and leaves the wall. The entrance (`fixed`) stays. `plan.ask` is set when it needs a yes first: `opensPens` floods a copy of the grid without those fences and names any exhibit whose animals would end up in a region reaching the map edge, or a building has `ask`. The view marks `planTiles(plan)` in red and shows the money back while dragging. After any paint or bulldoze, `replan` sends guests, animals and staff (`reroute`) whose route crosses a changed tile they can no longer stand on to pick again.

## Moving (world.js)
`moveBuilding(id, x, y)` is free and keeps the building's id and contents (a shed's stock). `spotProblem(type, x, y, skip)` is the shared fit check with `place`: on the map, bare dry ground (the moved building's own tiles count as free), nobody standing there, all inside one exhibit or all outside it, feeders inside and the rest outside. The entrance (`fixed`) doesn't move. Walkers whose route crossed the old or new tiles replan, and guests heading for a moved snack stand pick again. Fenced exhibits stay where their fence is. In the view, Move picks up a building on press, shows it as a ghost (red where it won't fit) while dragging, and puts it down on release.

## Placing (world.js, view/)
`placeProblem(type, x, y)` is read only and says why `place` would refuse (unique, `spotProblem`, money), or "". With a building tool on, the view keeps `ui.ghost` (`{type, x, y, ok, id?}`) under the cursor, centred the way a click puts it down (`placeCorner`): the renderer tints the footprint white or red and draws a see-through copy of the building over it (`drawBuilding`, shared with the real ones), and the status bar shows the reason. Move uses the same ghost (with the moved building's `id`, which the renderer then skips). Moving an animal uses a crate ghost instead: the animal panel's Move (`ui.startDrop`) turns on the `crate` tool (`TOOL_KIND` "drop", no menu button), `ui.ghost = {crate: id, x, y, ok}` follows the cursor, red with `dropProblem`'s reason in the status bar, and a click calls `moveAnimal` and goes back to Select. Touch has no hover, so on phones the ghost only shows after a tap.

## Exhibits (exhibits.js)
- `findRegions` flood-fills the tiles that aren't fence. A region that doesn't touch the map edge is an exhibit.
- `rebuildExhibits` gives each one an id by voting with the grid's `ex` layer: biggest regions vote first, so a merge keeps the bigger pen's exhibit; a split half without its own id copies the biome of the exhibit it came from. An exhibit whose pen is opened waits in `state.openPens` and comes back when the gap closes.
- `tally` counts land, shallow and deep tiles, fences, gates, chutes, feeders, props (enrichment buildings) and viewing spots (path tiles next to the pen's fence: every fence is see-through), and works out `security` (the weakest fence tile's tier) and `weakest`.
- `tileTier`: a fence type's tier, one lower when Damaged (Worn keeps it). Electric: 6 with power, 2 without, 1 without and Worn; Damaged electric has lost its power.

## Habitat (habitat.js)
`habitatScore(state, species, tally, count)` gives biome (preferred 1, acceptable 0.7, else 0.2), space (tiles per animal), water (share of the pen, sea water only counts behind aquarium or lagoon walls), group (inside min and max), and their average.

## Animals (animals.js)
Needs (`NEEDS`: hunger, thirst, enrich; `needsOf` drops thirst for swimmers) drain every step at `ANIMAL.fullDays` (config.js, in park days) times the species' `drain`. A species' `speed` (default 1, the T. rex has 2) multiplies its walk, `SPEED.animal`. Resting is not a need: the `rest` action scores `ANIMAL.lie.awake` or, outside the species' `active` hours (`ANIMAL.daylight`), `lie.asleep`, plus a random roll, against wander's 25 to 35. Behavior is the `ACTIONS` table: each action has `score(a, ctx)`, `start(a, ctx)` (false if it can't route there) and `finish(a, ctx)`, with `ctx = { state, derived, bus, s, t, ex }`, and optionally `faces(state, derived, a, tile)`: once there, `faceTo` turns it east or west to the first neighbouring tile that matches (eat: its feeder, drink: water). When an action runs out, its `finish` applies, then `choose` starts the best-scoring one that can start: test (when its danger is over the pen's security, likelier when bored), eat at a feeder (a meal takes off its share of a day's hunger, so a species still eats its `food` a day), drink, rest (lie down), play at an enrichment prop (`t.props`: buildings with `enrich`), maul (a species of `size` `ANIMAL.big` or more, enrichment need over `maulFrom`: goes to a random fence tile and wears it by `WEAR.mauledPerHour` while there, never breaking it outright; bell once a day), wander. An action may also have `arrived(a, ctx)`, called each step it stands with time left, and `clockMoving(s)`, true when its time also runs while walking: wander uses both for species with `cruise` (the Anomalocaris and the Mosasaurus), which head for another spot each time they arrive instead of standing, so a swimmer never parks mid-wander; land animals still walk to a spot and stand there. A new action is one entry. `testFence` wears the weakest tile and, after `WEAR.breakHours`, emits `breakFence`; the world clears the tile and `settleAnimals` marks anyone now outside an exhibit as escaped, except swimmers (`moves: "swim"`), which can't leave the water: they keep their `ex` and wait while the pen sits in `state.openPens`, and only a pen forgotten entirely crates them. Where animals may live is `fitProblem(sp, biome)` (habitat.js): a `sea` species needs a biome with `sea`, and nothing else may go in one. `buyAnimal`, `dropProblem`, `uncrate` and `setBiome` refuse on it, and the exhibit panel hides or disables what doesn't fit.
Crates: a crated animal (`a.crated`) is a box on the map at its `x, y`, with `a.ex` the exhibit it sits in (kept by `settleAnimals`) or 0, or riding with a keeper (`a.carried`, the keeper's id; its `x, y` follow the keeper). Its needs and wellbeing wait, and `animalsIn` leaves it out (it doesn't count for group, habitat, draw or a pen's danger). Crates come from `crate(id)` (an animal in an exhibit, boxed where it stands), a guard's dart (`caught`, always boxed where it was caught), a swimmer whose pen is gone, and the Genetics Lab (`crateBeside`: the nearest free open tile beside the lab, else the entrance; `migrate` puts old saves' off-map crates there too). `uncrate(id)` lets it out where it sits, if that's in an exhibit it fits and it can stand there (`uncrateProblem`). `sellAnimal(id)` takes any animal or crate that isn't loose or being carried, pays `sellPrice` (`ANIMAL.sell` of the species' price, booked as `sold`) and drops its jobs. `moveAnimal(id, x, y)` posts a keeper's haul job (below); `dropProblem` says why it can't (fences, buildings, open deep water, another crate, a pen without a keeper gate at either end, its own pen for a live animal, a misfit or a tile it can't stand on); a spot outside any exhibit is fine: the crate is stored there. `cancelMove` calls it off until it's picked up.
Hourly, `updateWellbeing`: stress eases toward a target from needs over 70 (weighted by `ANIMAL.stress.weights`: boredom half), the habitat score and being loose (`ANIMAL.stress`); health falls as hunger or thirst climbs past `starveFrom` (0 at 90, the full `starve` rate at 100) and, for species under `ANIMAL.big`, as boredom climbs past it (`boredSick`), and under high stress, and heals when calm (`ANIMAL.health`), with a bell ("is sickly.") under `weak`; at 0 it emits `animalDied` and the world removes the animal and its jobs (`died`, counted in `books.deaths`). Happiness is 45% habitat, 35% needs met, 20% calm.

## Staff and the job board (staff.js)
`planJobs` (hourly) posts feed jobs for pens whose feeders are under half full, and repair jobs for Worn or Damaged fence tiles. Each job has a key so it's posted once. Idle staff claim the highest-priority job of their role they can route to. Feeding: fetch from the nearest stocked shed, then deliver at a feeder through a gate, or, for a pen holding danger 3 or more, from outside beside a chute (staff never walk into a dangerous pen). A job that can't be done gets a `why` ("No feeding chute.") and waits an hour.
Guards take catch jobs, posted for every escaped animal: they follow it, dart it (`STAFF.dartMin`), and the world boxes it where it was caught, to wait until it's sent somewhere.
Keepers take haul jobs (`{kind: "haul", animal, ex, tile}`, from `moveAnimal`): `fetch` walks to the animal or its crate (following a live one that wanders), or, for a swimmer, a dangerous animal, a pen holding dangerous animals, or a tile staff cannot stand on, to just outside one of its gates; `load` holds it for `STAFF.loadMin`, crates it if it isn't already and picks it up (`s.carry = {crate}`); `deliver` uses the same gate rule, including the incoming animal even when the destination pen is empty. `unload` checks the chosen tile against the animal’s movement and biome rules (so deep water is valid for a swimmer), the exhibit id and other crates. If the destination changed during the journey, the keeper leaves the crate where they stand and reports why; the animal stays boxed. Otherwise it is set down on the chosen tile and released inside an exhibit. A keeper who loses the way, loses the job or is fired sets the crate down where they stand (`setDown`), and the job waits for the next keeper.

## Guests (guests.js)
Arrivals depend on rating, ticket price and how much the viewable exhibits draw. A guest pays the ticket, then: a need over 70 -> a building whose `serves` matches (hunger -> snack stand, bladder -> restroom; the worst one with a stand it can reach wins), and a guest who means to buy a souvenir (`GUESTS.giftChance`) and has seen something -> a gift stand, once. Stand sale and cost are data (`sale`, `cost`). Benches (`serves: "tired"`, `stay` minutes sitting) answer tiredness, which builds over `GUESTS.tiredMin`. A guest who buys a snack is left holding rubbish (`litter` 71) and wants a trash bin; with none in reach it drops it on the path (`state.litter`, swept every night until custodians exist), and each leaving guest loses up to half a star as that count nears `GUESTS.litterMax`. Unreached needs leave the guest unhappy: hunger, bladder or tiredness over 80 costs half a star. Otherwise the most appealing exhibit not seen yet (watch a few minutes, gain fun from its draw); out of time or exhibits -> back to the entrance, where they rate the visit and leave. A guest within `STAFF.panicTiles` of a loose animal of danger 3 or more runs for the exit and rates the visit half a star.

## Sandbox (money.js, science.js)
`state.sandbox` (the HUD's Sandbox button, `setSandbox`) is unlimited money for testing. Every money check goes through `afford(state, n)` and every charge through `charge(state, n)`: in sandbox mode everything is affordable and the bank isn't touched (income still comes in), while the books still record the costs. The HUD shows ∞, with the real bank in its tooltip. Sandbox also makes the labs instant: trips and clones take no time (`tripDays`, `cloneMinutes` return 0), a research step finishes on the next science tick, and the commands that start them (`scout`, `expedition`, `clone`, `assign`) run `instant()`, a zero-length `scienceTick`, so they land even while paused. Star requirements (research steps, expeditions) go through `hasStars`, which sandbox always passes.

## Time (world.js)
`advance(minutes)` runs fixed steps of `STEP_MIN`. Every hour: exhibits re-tallied, happiness, job planning. At `DAY_MIN` the night runs: wages, the nightly food delivery to each shed (yesterday's eating plus a margin, paid now), fence wear, the books.

## Saving (save.js)
JSON, grid layers as base64. `migrate()` fills in missing fields for older saves. `main.js` autosaves to localStorage every 20 seconds and on page hide.

## Build menu (view/menu.js)
`TOOL_KIND` names every tool and how it takes the pointer. `BuildMenu` fills `#build`: small Bulldoze and Move buttons side by side, then four big category buttons (Buildings, Landscaping, Exhibits, Paths), down from the top left (the bell sits beside the column). A category opens its tray along the bottom (menu A); an entry holding a list (Fence Types, Water, each building group) opens it as a bar above (menu B), one at a time. Everything is built from data: fences from `FENCES`, buildings by their `menu` group (guest, staff, exhibits) with `tip` as the tooltip, prices from data.js and `PRICE`. Select is always on underneath: a tool from a tray or list lasts only while its tray and list are open (`showing`), so closing them (×, Esc, another list or category) goes back to Select, and opening a category drops Bulldoze or Move. Tapping the tool that's on also goes back to Select. The map cursor shows the tool: an arrow for Select, a hand for Move, a crosshair for the rest. Esc drops Bulldoze or Move, else closes the list, then the tray, then the bell, then the panel. Icons are drawn placeholders until the menu art lands.

## The labs (science.js, view/labs.js)
- `state.science`: `done` (finished research step ids), `tracks` (track id -> `{prog}` in researcher-minutes), `crew` (`{id, role, track}`: researchers and geneticists, not walkers), `drones`, `seen` (site id -> scouted), `found` (species discovered), `genome` (species -> %), `trips` and `clones` (`{slot, start, end}` in park minutes, `nowMin`; `slot` is the drone or incubator number, the lowest free one, `freeSlot`), `reports` (the last `REPORTS_KEPT` trip reports, `{id, kind, site, slot, day, lines: [text, big]}`, until `dismissReport`). Defaults in `newScience`; `migrate` marks fence research done for fence types an older save already has, and numbers older trips and clones.
- Labs are buildings with `lab` and `unique` (data.js). Tap one to open its panel. Work stops without its lab (`trackWhy`, `tripProblem`, `cloneProblem`); bulldozing one keeps its crew and drones.
- Research: `TRACKS` (data.js) are straight lines of steps. Each step takes `stepMinutes(i)` (`SCIENCE.research`: first step a day for one researcher, each next 1.6 times longer), divided among the researchers on it, and waits for its `stars`. A step's `effect` adds up once done (`effect(state, key)`: `droneCut`, `tripTime`, `cloneTime`, `wearCut`); fences name their step in `tech` (`hasTech`), and `paint` refuses a locked one. The menu greys locked tools and built one-a-park buildings (`BuildMenu.refresh`). `assign(track, -1, id)` takes that researcher off.
- Time Travel Lab: `SITES` and `PERIODS` (data.js; sites from v1's `tools/timeline/sites.py`, shown while one of their `fauna` is in `SPECIES`). `EPOCHS` flattens every period's `epochs` (each ends where the next begins, the last at `TIMELINE_FLOOR`) with its map (`TIMELINE_MAPS`, `art/timeline/<name>.png`, 260 x 130 from v1). The timeline slider runs `u` from 0 (oldest) to 1 (today): one equal bar a period, split equally into its epochs, a log scale of years inside each (`yearsAt`, `uAt`). A site shows on the map of any epoch its window touches (`sitesIn`, marker at `at[map]`). It comes with one drone; more cost `nextDronePrice`. Each trip takes a drone for the period's `days` (`siteTripDays`). A scout lights the site and spots each unseen animal with `SCIENCE.scoutFind` by size (`sizeOf`: body length on a log scale); once all are found it's `siteComplete`. An expedition needs the species found and the park at its `stars`, succeeds with `tripOdds` and adds `100 / trips` % of genome, give or take `spread`. Results go to the bell and to a report.
- Genetics Lab: each geneticist runs an incubator (`incubators`). A clone needs a complete genome, costs the species' price and takes `cloneMinutes`; it hatches into a crate beside the lab (`crateBeside`). The lab's Crates tab lists every crate in the park and where it sits, with the same Move, Uncrate and Sell buttons as the animal panel (`ui.crateBtns`); Sell asks "Are you sure?" in the row first (`ui.sellAsk`, `sellSure` in labs.js). Only `starter` species can be bought outright.
- Every night `scienceBill` pays crew wages and lab `upkeep`.
- The panels (labs.js, after v1's scienceui.js) open full size over the map (`#side.full`, beside the build column; all of it on phones) with sections in two columns (`fullCols`; one column under 1000 px). Tab bars (`tabBar`, `data-tab`) pick the research track, the Time Travel Lab's Lab/Reports, the Genetics Lab's Cloning/Crates and the genome library's period (`PERIOD_COLOR` in data.js); the picks live on the UI (`rsTrack`, `ttTab`, `gnTab`, `gnPeriod`), not in the save. Research: researchers, a bay card a researcher (its step, bar, Stop) and the track's steps as cards (Researched, Research/Add researcher, or locked with what it needs; what a step does comes from its `effect` and fence). Time Travel Lab: a drone card a drone (`slot`), the timeline (epoch head, SVG map with site markers, the slider, site chips, PALEOMAP credit) and the open site's card (`ttSite`: When, Trip, a Target / Genome / Survey Success table with a radio pick `ttPick`, Send expedition and Send scouting trip). Dragging the slider, its keys (arrows, Page Up/Down a whole epoch, Home/End) and map taps (nearest marker within 14 map px) redraw the timeline in place (`ttRefresh`, which also sets `ui.lastPanel` so the refresh loop leaves it), and `ui.holdPanel` stops rebuilds while dragging. Genetics Lab: geneticists, an incubator card a geneticist (`slot`), the genome library. Bars and times that move with the clock carry `data-p`/`data-left` (`track:`, `trip:`, `clone:`) and are filled by `labLive` after every refresh, so the panel's HTML only changes when something happens.
